🪐Bizarre World: Strange Exoplanets

1. HD 189733b (The Glass Rain Planet)

This deep blue exoplanet looks peaceful from afar, but it features screaming winds up to 5,400 mph and a weather system where it rains molten glass sideways across its atmosphere.

2. Diamond Planet (55 Cancri e)

This “super-Earth” is believed to be composed largely of carbon, which, under immense pressure and heat, exists largely as crystalline diamond. The planet's surface glistens and refracts light intensely, appearing like a colossal cut gemstone.

3. Gliese 436b (The Burning Ice Planet)

A Neptune-sized world where surface temperatures reach over 430°C. Despite the scorching heat, immense gravity compresses water into a solid form known as "ice-X" creating a planet covered in burning ice.
